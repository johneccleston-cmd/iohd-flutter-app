Tech Commission Rules

Starts Monday, Oct 5, 2026 · Pay week is Monday to Sunday

Your commission comes from the jobs you work and finish. These are the rules that decide what counts, when you are paid and what comes out.

What counts

  • Jobs that start on or after Oct 5. A job that started earlier is ignored completely, even if you work it after Oct 5. That includes callbacks on it.
  • Only verified days. A day counts when your Site Visit status is set to Completed. No Completed, no credit for that day.
  • Full day = 1, half day = 0.5. Verify Delivery, Manage Project and site check visits earn no credit.
  • The job's pool is split by days. After 1% goes to the company pool, 100% of what is left is divided among the techs with verified days on that job.

When you get paid

  • In the week the job closes, not the week you worked it. The job closes the first time it reaches a closed status.
  • Fast pay: on a service job or a residential install, if all your work was in one pay week and the job closes by 5:00 pm the following Monday, you are paid for the week you worked it. Commercial installs never get fast pay.
  • Close it late, get paid late. A job that closes after that cutoff, or that takes work in 2 or more pay weeks, is paid in its close week.

The $750 weekly cut

  • Each week the company keeps the first $750 of your weekly total. You are paid the total minus $750, and never less than $0.
  • Your weekly total = closed job pay + advances + retainage held + callback pay.
  • Under $750 pays $0 for that week. Nothing rolls over to the next week.

Long jobs, advances and retainage

  • Multi-week jobs: each verified day before the close week pays a flat $200 advance in the week you worked it. Advances count toward the $750.
  • Commercial installs: a verified day also pays the $200 advance if the job is still open when that week's Friday ends, even if it is not a multi-week job.
  • When the job closes, your advances are taken back out of that job's pay only.
  • Commercial retainage: 20% of a commercial install's pay is held. It counts toward your $750 that week and is paid out later (see below).
  • Quarterly retainage payout: held retainage is paid one full quarter after the quarter the job started in. Q4 2026 retainage is the first payout, on Apr 1, 2027. Q1 pays Jul 1, Q2 pays Oct 1, Q3 pays Jan 1, Q4 pays Apr 1.

Callbacks and strikes

  • Callback pay: a verified callback or warranty day pays a flat $50 in the week you worked it. It is separate from the job pool.
  • No callback pay on service jobs, or on jobs for Lennar or Schuber Mitchell Homes.
  • A strike goes on every tech who did the original job each time a verified callback visit happens on it. Strikes are counted by calendar year.
  • The $50 is charged to the original techs' retainage, or to the company pool. Service jobs have no charge, but the strike still counts.

Example week

Closed jobs: $950 + one $200 advance day = $1,150 total
$1,150 − $750 = $400 paid

Every job

  1. Set your Site Visit to Completed the day you work.
  2. Mark callbacks and warranty visits as such in the visit.
  3. Tell the office if a day is missing or wrong.
